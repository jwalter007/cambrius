About Cambrius

Intelligence can emerge without a central brain.

What if the intelligence already distributed throughout a company could be connected without forcing everything into one monolithic system?

Created by Jason Walter

Plants sense, communicate, adapt and respond without a central processor directing every action. Their behavior emerges from distributed systems interacting with an environment.

Organizations face a similar problem. What a company knows is distributed across people, conversations, documents, software, relationships, decisions and accumulated experience.

Why the name?

Cambrius is inspired by cambium: the living layer in plants responsible for continuing growth. Useful intelligence should likewise continually incorporate new information, form connections and change as its environment changes.

Built from real systems

The architecture grew out of building domain-specific systems where information arrives naturally, accumulates over time and must become available to specialized AI processes without losing context, evidence or human control.

The objective

Systems that help organizations remember more of what they learn, understand how information relates, recognize meaningful changes and make accumulated knowledge useful at the moment it matters.
