Organizational Intelligence

A living intelligence layer for organizations.

Cambrius turns information scattered across people, conversations, documents and systems into connected knowledge an organization can remember, reason over and act upon.

Intelligence does not require a central brain. It can emerge from distributed signals, relationships and accumulated experience. Cambrius applies that idea to organizations.
The problem

Organizations know more than their software does.

Knowledge is fragmented

A CRM knows one piece. Email knows another. A person remembers why something happened. Useful intelligence exists between those systems.

Context disappears

Records rarely preserve changing confidence, provenance, relationships and history that determine what an organization should currently believe.

AI waits to be asked

Organizational intelligence should notice meaningful changes, contradictions, risks and opportunities nobody thought to query.
